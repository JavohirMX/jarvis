Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Jarvis

Which AI providers are supported?

Jarvis currently supports OpenAI (GPT-4), Anthropic (Claude), and Google (Gemini). You can switch between providers seamlessly or use multiple providers simultaneously.

Is my data secure?

Yes! All your data is stored locally on your device. API communications are encrypted, and we never store or transmit your personal data to our servers. Your API keys are securely stored in your local configuration.

What are the system requirements?

Jarvis requires a 64-bit processor, 4GB RAM (8GB recommended), and 200MB of storage space. It's compatible with Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, and major Linux distributions like Ubuntu 20.04+.

How do I get started?

Simply download Jarvis for your platform, install it, and configure your AI provider API keys in the settings. Check out our documentation for detailed setup instructions and best practices.
